Portal

Portal is the original game of the Portal series.
It is a single-player puzzle-based video game developed by Valve and released as part of The Orange Box on October 9, 2007 for Microsoft Windows and Xbox 360;
the PlayStation 3 version released at the later date of December 11, 2007.
Portal was also re-released as part of the launch of Mac OS X support for Steam on May 12, 2010;
to celebrate, it was given away for free until May 24, 2010 to Steam users on a promotional page on the Steam website.
Further to this, Portal has been made available for free again for a limited duration as part of the Learn With Portals project in 2011.

Story

The player takes on the role of Chell, a test subject in the Aperture Science Enrichment Center.
Chell is guided through a series of test chambers by GLaDOS, an artificial intelligence that controls the facility.
The player must solve puzzles using the "Aperture Science Handheld Portal Device", a device that can create inter-spatial portals between two flat planes.
The story is told through GLaDOS' voice, which provides instructions and commentary throughout the game.

Characters

  • Chell - The silent protagonist and player character of the game.
  • GLaDOS - The main antagonist of the game, an artificial intelligence that controls the Aperture Science facility.

Game Mechanics

The game is played from a first-person perspective, and the player must navigate through a series of test chambers using the portal gun to create portals on flat surfaces.
The player can use the portals to move themselves and objects around the environment, solve puzzles, and reach otherwise inaccessible areas.
The game also features a unique physics engine that allows for creative problem-solving and experimentation with the portal mechanics.
