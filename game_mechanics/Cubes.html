Cubes

Cubes are interactive objects in the Portal series that can be moved, stacked, and used to solve puzzles. They are typically made of a durable material and can be pushed, pulled, and placed in various configurations to create platforms or activate switches.

Types of Cubes

Weighted Storage Cube

The Weighted Storage Cube was first introduced in Portal, and remains prevalent in Portal 2. In Portal 2 however, the cube is given a blue ring in the center instead of a blue cross, and can turn yellow when placed upon a Super Button.

Companion Cube

The Companion Cube is a special type of Weighted Storage Cube that is introduced in Portal. It is distinguished by its heart-shaped markings and is often used in puzzles that require the player to carry it through test chambers. The Companion Cube has become an iconic symbol of the Portal series, representing companionship and emotional attachment.

Discouragement Redirection Cube

The Discouragement Redirection Cube was introduced in Portal 2. It's main purpose is to redirect Thermal Discouragement Beams. It can also act as a Weighted Storage Cube to activate Heavy-Duty Super Buttons.

Edgeless Safety Cube

The Edgeless Safety Cube is a type of cube that was introduced in Portal 2. It is characterized by its smooth, edgeless design and is often used in puzzles that require precise placement. The cube's unique shape makes it useful for creating stable platforms and solving complex mechanical challenges. It appears in various test chambers during the co-op campaign.

Frankenturret

The Frankenturret is a type of cube that was introduced in Portal 2. They consist of a combination of two turret heads and legs fused with a Weighted Storage Cube. Wheatley created them as a replacement for the player. Instead of solving the chambers themselves they will aimlessly hop around while not placed on a button. If fallen over they start waving their legs trying to get up.
