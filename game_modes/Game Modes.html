Story Mode

The Story Mode is the single player campaign included in both Portal and Portal 2. It includes all the levels, test chambers and boss fights created by the developers.

Portal

The Story Mode of Portal includes a total of 20 Test Chambers, numbered from 00 to 19.
Besides the test chambers there are 4 Portal Escape Parts, GLaDOS's chamber (boss fight) and Still Alive (credits)

Portal 2

The second Portal game is seperated into 10 chapters, spread over 5 Acts which each include a different amount of chambers.

Chapter 1 (Act 1): The Courtesy Call

After centuries in cryosleep, Chell awakens in a dilapidated Aperture Science facility. She encounters Wheatley, a clumsy personality core, who helps her retrieve the portal gun and search for a way to escape the ruins.

Chapter 2 (Act 2) Cold Boot:

While attempting to escape, Chell and Wheatley accidentally reactivate GLaDOS—an AI previously thought to be dead. She immediately separates the two and forces Chell to complete new and repaired test chambers while she rebuilds the facility.

Chapter 3 (Act 2) The Return

GLaDOS subjects Chell to increasingly complex test chambers featuring new mechanics, such as Aerial Faith Plates and lasers, all the while sarcastically venting her resentment regarding the events of the first game.

Chapter 4 (Act 2) The surprise:

Just before GLaDOS can trigger a supposed "surprise" (a deadly trap), Wheatley bursts through the wall of the test chamber. Together, they escape behind the scenes of the testing facilities into the maintenance areas.

Chapter 5 (Act 2) The Escape:

Chell and Wheatley sabotage GLaDOS's defense systems by replacing the production of sentry turrets with defective models and cutting off her neurotoxin supply. Ultimately, they confront GLaDOS in her chamber and replace her with Wheatley as the new central core.

Chapter 6 (Act 3) The Fall:

Corrupted by the power of the central core, Wheatley turns against Chell and casts her—along with GLaDOS, who has been relegated to a potato battery—into the deepest, abandoned layers of Aperture Science dating back to the 1950s.

Chapter 7 (Act 3) The Reunion:

On their way back up, Chell and GLaDOS traverse old testing areas—navigating them with the help of pre-recorded messages from company founder Cave Johnson (and his assistant, Caroline)—while making use of various testing gels.

Chapter 8 (Act 4) The Itch:

Back in the upper facility, they discover that Wheatley is losing control and the entire complex is on the verge of a meltdown. He forces Chell through hastily assembled, chaotic test chambers in order to satisfy his programmed "itch to test."

Chapter 9 (Act 4) The Part Where He Kills You:

Wheatley attempts to kill Chell and GLaDOS by trapping them, but they manage to escape into his main chamber. In the final battle, Chell uses a portal to the moon to suck Wheatley out into space; GLaDOS regains control, deletes her human memories, and ultimately releases Chell to the Earth's surface.

Chapter 10 (Act 5) The Credits

Co-Op Mode

The Co-Op Mode is the only multiplayer mode. It was introduced in Portal 2 along with new characters and objects.

GLaDOS created Atlas and P-body as replacements for the test subject Chell which GLaDOS allowed to leave at the end of the story mode. The two robots each have a Handheld Portal Device, unlike the Story Mode the Co-Op Mode allows for the placement of 4 Portals in total (2 by each player). Also the color of the Portals changed, each robot placing a seperate colored pair. With the dynamic of two players, new puzzle objects and the increased amount of Portals the Co-Op Mode brought a new feeling into the portal expierience.

Community Test Chambers

Another new Mode introduced in Portal 2 were the Community Test Chambers. Test Chambers created by players using the build-in editor from Portal 2, allowing to place panels, buttons, lasers and portals without needing complex external level-design tools.
