Hoopy the hoop

Background

Hoopy the hoop is a character which fell from the sky among the wreckage of GLaDOS at the end of the game. It landed on the Aperture Science parking lot in front of Chell, right before she got dragged away. It is more of a joke character than a serious participant in the story.

Appearances

  • Portal (2007): Hoopy the hoop rolls across the screen at the end of the game as Chell lays outside the Aperture Science facility.
  • Portal 2 (2011): Hoopy the hoop appears a few times throughout the game, 4 times in total.

Abilities

Hoopy the hoop does not posess any abilities. As a joke character, it serves no functional purpose in the game's mechanics.

Personality

Hoopy the hoop does not have a personality, as it is an inanimate object. Its role is primarily comedic, adding a touch of whimsy to the game's narrative.
