Caroline

Background

Caroline is a character in Portal 2, known for her role in the game's narrative and her interactions with the player character. She is a scientist at Aperture Science and plays a significant role in the story's development.

Appearances

  • Portal 2 (2011)

Abilities

Caroline possesses knowledge and skills related to science and technology, which she uses to contribute to the game's plot.

Personality

Caroline is characterized by her intelligence and dedication to her work. She is often portrayed as a supportive figure who helps the player navigate the challenges of the game.
